WHAT IS IDK.S?

It was literally called -idk.s because we didn't know what to put there. So we made the most fun “idk” on the internet.

Nine games, one page, zero backend. Runs at 60fps, works offline after first load, respects prefers-reduced-motion.

  • ⚡ Zero dependencies, pure vanilla JS + Canvas
  • 🎮 Built for keyboard, mouse, and touch
  • 🔊 Web Audio bleeps (toggle ♪)
  • 🏆 Daggerfall is real raycasting. Papers is real bureaucracy.
  • 🧱 Breakout, Vector Storm, Merge and Cipher Flash joined in v2.2
SPACE shoot / drop / inspect
