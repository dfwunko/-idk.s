WHAT IS IDK.S?

It was literally called -idk.s because we didn't know what to put there. So we made the most fun “idk” on the internet.

No tracking. No accounts. No energy system. Open source, runs at 60fps, works offline after first load, respects prefers-reduced-motion.

  • ⚡ Zero dependencies, pure vanilla JS + Canvas
  • 🎮 Built for keyboard, mouse, and touch
  • 🔊 Web Audio bleeps (toggle ♪)
  • 🏆 Global-ish daily counter (fake but motivating)
